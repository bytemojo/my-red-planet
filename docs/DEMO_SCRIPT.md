# 2-minute demo video: My Little Red Planet

For the team recording the NASA Space Apps submission video. Record the browser at 1280×800, sound on. Start a normal game and advance to the Storm Call before recording the timed sequence. Times are targets; the spoken lines are suggestions, keep them short and plain.

| Time | Show on screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | The **Storm Call** card appears: countdown bar, three choices, REAL DATA tag. | "A dust storm is about to bury your solar panels, and you have 12 seconds. Stow them and lose power tonight, or leave them out and risk losing a lot more." |
| 0:12–0:22 | Press **Ask Earth**. "Message sent… 3 to 22 minutes each way." Let the timer run out. | "You can ask Earth, but a radio message takes up to 22 minutes each way. By the time the answer comes, the storm has hit." |
| 0:22–0:35 | Sleep to Sol 2: morning report ("Dust buried the panels overnight"), then Reyes: "Earth's answer finally came in." | "Every choice has a consequence you can see. Out here, kids learn that crews have to decide for themselves." |
| 0:35–0:50 | Sofia's **radiation peak** card: water walls (cost water and power) / storage corner (morale) / hope. Point at the three meters and the SPACE WEATHER chip. | "The storm is modelled on a real X9.0 solar flare from NASA's DONKI database. Water makes a good radiation shield, but it is also your drinking water. No choice is free." |
| 0:50–1:05 | Back on the menu: tap the compact **Solar Forecast** warning to open its details and NASA source. | "When you are online, the game reads the last 30 days of solar flares from DONKI. The strongest flare sets the storm's modeled radiation alert; offline, the game labels its archived flare." |
| 1:05–1:25 | Fast montage: crew quarters (faces, food bubble, meal hatch), watering a bed, gym rowing machine unfolding, airlock "sealed", comms static. | "You also care for a sick crew, grow food, exercise to stay healthy, and keep air, water and power running. Two or three meters on screen; the rest happens in the world." |
| 1:25–1:45 | The **debrief**: stars, four metrics, and the decision → consequence tree. Press **Replay with a different plan**. | "At the end you see exactly what your choices caused, and you can replay with a different plan. Failing is part of learning, not a punishment." |
| 1:45–2:00 | Journal → **Mars map** pin at Hebrus Valles; then `docs/NASA_SOURCES.md` scrolling. | "The base sits at a real place, Hebrus Valles. Every NASA data point and link in the game is listed for you here, and the game says clearly what is real data and what is a game model." |

## Tips
- Before recording: Settings → sound on; close other tabs; let the menu finish loading the DONKI card (a few seconds).
- To reach Sol 2 quickly during recording: open the Control Module (**C**) → Time → Rest until morning.
- Do not claim the game measures radiation on Mars: DONKI records solar flares near Earth; the alert levels are a game model.
- If possible, film a real 7–12-year-old playing for a few seconds and use one honest quote.
